# Alex Snow School - LLM Context File

> A Youths' run school for empowering the next generation towards Myanmar economic and technological advancement.

## About Alex Snow School

Alex Snow School is an educational initiative focused on bridging the skills gap for fresh STEM graduates in Myanmar through peer-to-peer community learning approaches. The school provides free, high-quality courses in technology, economics, and finance to empower young professionals with modern technical skills and financial literacy.

**Website:** https://alexsnowschool.github.io
**Location:** Yangon, Myanmar
**Contact:** alexsnow348@gmail.com

### Mission

The project addresses two main challenges facing technological university graduates in Myanmar:
1. Need for quality education, especially in rural technical universities
2. Lack of experiences and required up-to-date technological skill sets

The school also aims to enhance financial literacy among millennials and young professionals for better personal finance and investment decisions.
